01Bay 01 — The basics

What is a food truck commissary?

A commissary is a health-department-approved base where mobile food vendors prep food, store supplies, refill water and propane, dispose of waste and clean their units between shifts.

Houston requires mobile food units to operate from an approved commissary. El Castillo is on the City of Houston's approved list, and unlike most, we never close.

06Bay 06 — Questions

Straight answers.

Still have a question? Call us any hour: (346) 254-2525

Do food trucks in Houston need a commissary?

Yes. The City of Houston requires mobile food units to operate from a health-department-approved commissary, and Texas law requires units in large counties like Harris to return to their commissary within the 24 hours before operating. El Castillo is on the City of Houston's approved list.

Are you really open 24 hours?

Yes. El Castillo is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, so you can service your truck before a breakfast shift or after a late-night close.

Can I refill propane at El Castillo?

Yes. Propane refills are available on site at 6345 Skyline Dr, Houston, TX 77057.

Do you build food trucks?

Yes. We build custom food trucks from the frame up (layout, kitchen build-out and equipment) around your concept and menu.

Where is El Castillo Commissary?

6345 Skyline Dr, Houston, TX 77057, in Mid West Houston, a few minutes from the Galleria.
